# Research requirements [PVE-GOV-001]

## Authority [PVE-GOV-001]
GitHub accepted state is authoritative; candidate, memory and chat context cannot silently replace accepted project state.

## Source provenance [PVE-SRC-001]
Research claims are bound to exact source provenance; ambiguous extraction yields to the rendered primary source.

## Sequential audit [PVE-AUD-001]
The complete book is audited in source order and every substantive section is dispositioned.

## Mathematical verification [PVE-MATH-001]
Strong mathematical status requires explicit assumptions, units and reproducible derivation/proof evidence.

## Physics validation [PVE-PHY-001]
Mathematical validity, physical interpretation and relation to comparator theories are distinct questions.

## Experimental evidence [PVE-EVD-001]
Empirical support is evaluated against quantitative comparator predictions and uncertainty.

## Status promotion [PVE-STATE-001]
Scientific status is multi-axis and cannot be promoted strongly without registered evidence on the relevant axis.
